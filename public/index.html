fairshake

Hold the money. Check the work.
Pay for what was delivered.

Small freelance jobs usually end in one of two bad ways: the client pays upfront and hopes, or the freelancer works first and hopes. Fairshake puts the money on hold with PayPal, lets an AI reviewer check the delivery line by line against the brief, and then captures only the part that was actually done. The rest goes back automatically.

1. AgreePaste the brief. AI turns it into a weighted checklist you both can see.
2. HoldClient approves in PayPal. Funds are authorized, not charged.
3. DeliverFreelancer pastes the work or a link. AI checks each item with evidence.
4. SettleClient confirms. PayPal captures the earned share, voids the rest.
